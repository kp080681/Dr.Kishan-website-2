## Executive Summary

The website has a strong approved content and route base: a polished homepage, 16 condition pages, 5 public patient-education blog articles, approved consultation/location data, real book/publication assets, and clear doctor/credential data in `src/content/site.ts`.

The main SEO risk before indexing is technical metadata infrastructure: no sitemap, no robots route/file, no canonical handling, no metadata base, no Twitter metadata, no Open Graph images, and no structured data/schema. The condition pages are indexable but currently have thin/generic metadata and very short page content by design. These should be handled carefully in later SEO nodes without rewriting approved public content or creating thin/location-spam pages.
